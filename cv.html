Research Projects

Deception in Discrete-Time Linear-Quadratic Control

Aug 2025–present

Georgia Tech · CORE Lab · Advisor: Prof. Matthew Hale

Deceptive controllers for discrete-time linear-quadratic systems under adversarial detection constraints, based on sequential hypothesis testing. Derived semi-explicit optimal controllers with well-posedness guarantees, with analytical and Monte Carlo methods to characterize detection probability.

Metrics and Tactics for Deception in Sequential Decision Systems

May 2024–Feb 2026

Georgia Tech · CORE Lab · Advisor: Prof. Matthew Hale

Regularized policy-optimization formulations for three forms of deception in multi-agent MDPs against inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) adversaries. Derived performance-loss bounds and evaluated the strategies on a 5-agent MDP with 248K+ joint state-action pairs using three IRL methods.

MPC-Based Block-Lifting Control of Floating Cranes

Mar 2021–Jun 2022

Seoul National University · System Design Laboratory · Advisor: Prof. Myung-Il Roh

Constrained model predictive control for underactuated floating-crane block lifting under marine disturbances, with an augmented controller that compensates disturbances online using next-step prediction.

Design of Distributed SVM Algorithm using Blended Dynamics Approach

Apr–Dec 2021

Seoul National University · Control and Dynamic Systems Laboratory · Advisor: Prof. Hyungbo Shim

Consensus-based algorithm for distributed support vector machines in private, online learning settings, with a distributed optimization method for the dual SVM validated in MATLAB.

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) of Robots

Aug 2020–Feb 2021

Seoul National University · CAD and IT Laboratory · Advisor: Prof. Tae-Wan Kim

LiDAR-based graph SLAM in Python using scan matching, loop closure, and pose-graph optimization to estimate robot trajectories and reconstruct environment maps.
