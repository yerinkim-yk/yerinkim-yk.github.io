I am a Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. Matthew Hale in the Control, Optimization, and Robotics Engineering (CORE) Lab.

My research focuses on autonomous decision-making, control, and optimization, particularly how multi-agent systems balance competing objectives. I develop policies and controllers that account for adversarial inference while maintaining task performance.

Previously, I worked on robotic perception at ABB Robotics and machine learning recommendation systems at Blux. I received my B.S. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering from Seoul National University.

Research Projects

Deception in Discrete-Time Linear-Quadratic Control

Georgia Tech · CORE Lab · Aug 2025–present

Developed deceptive controllers for discrete-time linear-quadratic systems under adversarial detection constraints using sequential hypothesis testing.

  • Derived semi-explicit optimal controllers with well-posedness guarantees.
  • Developed analytical and Monte Carlo methods to characterize the probability of detection.

Metrics and Tactics for Deception in Sequential Decision Systems

Georgia Tech · CORE Lab · May 2024–Feb 2026

Developed regularized policy-optimization formulations for three forms of deception in multi-agent Markov decision processes (MDPs) against inverse reinforcement learning (IRL) adversaries.

  • Derived formal bounds on the performance loss induced by deception.
  • Evaluated three deception strategies in a five-agent MDP with over 248,000 joint state-action pairs using three IRL methods.

MPC-Based Block-Lifting Control of Floating Cranes

Seoul National University · Mar 2021–Jun 2022

Formulated constrained model predictive control for underactuated floating-crane block lifting under marine disturbances, with online disturbance compensation using next-step disturbance prediction.

Design of Distributed SVM Algorithm using Blended Dynamics Approach

Seoul National University · Apr–Dec 2021

Proposed a consensus-based distributed SVM algorithm for private, online learning. Derived a distributed optimization method for the dual SVM and validated convergence behavior in MATLAB.

Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) of Robots

Seoul National University · Aug 2020–Feb 2021

Implemented LiDAR-based graph SLAM in Python using scan matching, loop closure, and pose-graph optimization to estimate robot trajectories and reconstruct environment maps.
